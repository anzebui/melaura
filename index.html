Part 1 · The product

What is Melaura dry milk?

Fresh organic milk with the water and fat gently removed. What stays is the protein, calcium and taste — in a light powder you mix back with water whenever you need it.

🌱

Organic

Made from certified organic milk, without artificial additives or preservatives.

⏳

Durable

Stays good for over a year unopened — no fridge needed until you mix it.

♻️

Less waste

Mix only what you need. No half-empty cartons going sour in the fridge.

💪

Protein & calcium

Non-fat and naturally rich in protein — great for baking and sports nutrition.

Our approach

Six secrets to successful market entry

Based on the paper “Secrets to Successful New Market Entry” — and how Melaura applies each idea.

  1. 01

    Treat the new market like a start-up

    Small team, fast decisions, lean budget. We test, learn and adjust before scaling.

  2. 02

    Avoid new-market panic

    Slow first months are expected. We set realistic goals and don't drop prices in a rush.

  3. 03

    Gut feel works if you get the facts

    Market research on similar new products in Lithuania before we commit to big decisions.

  4. 04

    Succeed by making mistakes

    Pilot with a few bakeries and stores, collect feedback and improve quickly.

  5. 05

    Bet big but stay focused

    One product, two clear segments — baking and sports — instead of trying to reach everyone.

  6. 06

    Invest in your stakeholders

    Strong relationships with suppliers, retailers, advisors and influencers who know the market.
